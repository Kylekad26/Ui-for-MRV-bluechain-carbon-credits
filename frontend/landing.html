How It Works
From satellite scene to blockchain proof in under 3 minutes.
01
🛠️

Sentinel-2 Ingestion

Drop a lat/lon pin on the Sundarbans delta. BlueChain queries the ESA STAC API for the latest Sentinel-2 L2A scene, validates cloud cover (<30%), and extracts NDVI from Band 4 and Band 8 reflectance.

02
🌱

Random Forest Estimation

A 300-estimator Random Forest — trained on Sundarbans field survey data — predicts carbon density (tC/ha) from NDVI, latitude, and longitude. AGB and SOC stocks are split using Murdiyarso mangrove constants. GMW zone validation confirms the site is genuine mangrove.

03
⛓️

On-Chain Mint & Certificate

The oracle wallet calls verifyAndIssueCredits() on the BlueCarbonRegistry contract. BCO2 tokens are minted to your Aadhaar-linked wallet. An IPFS proof hash and a downloadable PDF certificate are generated instantly.
